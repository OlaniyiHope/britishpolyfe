About British Poly

Excellence in polytechnic education since 1987 — shaping the next generation of leaders and innovators.

Our Story

Founded in 1987, British Polytechnic has grown from a small technical college into one of the UK's leading polytechnic institutions. We have consistently delivered high-quality, practical education that prepares students for successful careers.

Our approach combines rigorous academic theory with hands-on, industry-aligned training. We work closely with employers, professional bodies, and community partners to ensure our graduates are ready to make an immediate impact in their chosen fields.

Today, we serve over 12,000 students across more than 60 programs, with a faculty of experienced professionals and researchers dedicated to student success.

Mission & Values

Our guiding principles that shape everything we do.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality polytechnic education that empowers students with practical skills, critical thinking, and a lifelong passion for learning. We strive to bridge the gap between education and industry, creating pathways to meaningful careers.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognised polytechnic institution that transforms lives through education, drives innovation, and contributes to economic and social development in our communities and beyond.

🎯

Excellence

We pursue excellence in teaching, research, and service, setting high standards for ourselves and our students.

🤝

Integrity

We act with honesty, transparency, and accountability in all our dealings with students, staff, and partners.

🌍

Diversity

We celebrate diversity and inclusion, creating a welcoming environment for people of all backgrounds.

Innovation

We embrace innovation and continuous improvement, adapting to the changing needs of students and industries.
