Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about British Polytechnic.

❓ How do I apply?
You can apply online through our admissions portal. Visit the Admissions page for detailed instructions and deadlines.
❓ What are the entry requirements?
Entry requirements vary by program. Generally, we require a high school diploma with a minimum GPA of 2.5 and English language proficiency (IELTS 6.0 or equivalent).
❓ What scholarships are available?
We offer a range of scholarships including merit-based, need-based, and international student scholarships. Contact our finance department for details.
❓ Can I study part-time or online?
Yes, we offer flexible learning options including part-time, online, and hybrid programs. Check the program pages for specific delivery modes.
❓ What support is available for international students?
We provide comprehensive support including visa assistance, language support, cultural integration programs, and dedicated international student advisors.
❓ How do I get a transcript?
Official transcripts can be requested through the Student Portal or by contacting the Registrar's Office. Processing takes 3-5 working days.
