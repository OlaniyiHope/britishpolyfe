Admissions

Your journey to British Polytechnic starts here. Find everything you need to know about applying, requirements, and key dates.

How to Apply

Follow these simple steps to start your application process.

1

Choose Your Program

Explore our programs and select the one that aligns with your career goals and interests.

2

Check Requirements

Review the entry requirements for your chosen program, including academic qualifications and English language proficiency.

3

Submit Application

Complete our online application form. Upload your supporting documents and pay the application fee.

4

Accept Offer

Receive your offer letter, accept your place, and complete your enrollment to secure your spot.

Entry Requirements

What you need to qualify for admission to British Polytechnic.

🎓

Academic Qualifications

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Minimum GPA of 2.5 (or equivalent)
  • Specific subject requirements for some programs
  • International qualifications assessed on a case-by-case basis
🌍

English Language Proficiency

  • IELTS: 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in each band)
  • TOEFL iBT: 80 overall
  • Cambridge English: C1 Advanced or C2 Proficiency
  • Duolingo English Test: 105+
📄

Supporting Documents

  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • Proof of English language proficiency
  • Personal statement or statement of purpose
  • Letters of recommendation (2 required)
  • CV or resume (for postgraduate applicants)
🛂

International Students

  • Valid passport and student visa
  • Proof of financial support
  • Health insurance coverage
  • CAS number from British Polytechnic
  • ATAS clearance for certain programs
